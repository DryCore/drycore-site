Founder and chief executive

Klifton Finley

Seventeen years in industrial extraction — designing, operating, and training teams on closed-loop hydrocarbon and CO₂ pressure systems inside classified hazardous-location facilities, under state licensing and inspection.

That is not a data-center résumé. It is the adjacent discipline. A refrigerant machine lives under the same rules: a closed circuit, a classified or inspected environment, a crew that does not get to improvise on a live loop, and a failure mode that is pressure before it is software.

The company is early. The standard it is using is not. Thresholds are written before the run. Claims wait for the instrument.

How the company behaves

Four operating rules.

01

Write the fail first

Pass and fail thresholds are published internally before the instrumented run. A result that misses does not get redescribed as a success.

02

Do not narrate a number

Energy, water, and noise stay off the public page until they have been measured against those thresholds.

03

Ask where it does not fit

Operator conversations include the sites where waterless rejection is the wrong tool. A mismatch found early is cheaper than a pilot.

04

IP before exposition

Provisional applications are filed. The mechanism is not a website diagram. Pending is stated as pending.
