Why a company can exist here

The buy is shifting from efficiency to permission.

Cooling equipment has long been bought on kilowatts and capital cost. AI density did not remove that test. It added two others that procurement cannot waive: water, because evaporation at campus scale is now a political fact, and noise, because the plant has neighbors.

Closed-loop liquid cooling inside the hall is advancing. Heat rejection at the wall is still where those three tests meet. A method that posts zero water, holds its energy number on a hot afternoon, and can be permitted is the purchase. DryCore has not posted that number. It is built to be allowed to try, under a threshold written in advance.

What would make the thesis true

  • WaterMeasured zero consumption at the rejection step, on the bench, against the pre-written threshold.
  • EnergyLower energy per watt rejected at high ambient than the dry-loop penalty operators already pay.
  • NoiseAn acoustic signature that a developer can take into a hearing.
  • FitAt least one operator who can name a site where those three, together, are the binding constraint.

Recommended capital sequence

The best strategy is to buy measurement, not narrative.

For a company at this stage, the rational path — for the founder and for an investor — is staged capital against technical gates. Raising a large priced round before a bench result transfers risk that should still sit in the experiment.

GATE 0 · NOW

Non-dilutive first

Submit the NSF SBIR Phase I proposal the program has invited. Use it, if awarded, to pay for instrumentation and the pre-registered bench — not for a commercial organization. Invitation is not funding.

GATE 1

Seed only against a written protocol

Outside capital, if taken before an award, should be small, instrument-backed, and tied to the same pass/fail sheet. Preferred form at this gate: a simple instrument (SAFE or note) with no story about revenue multiples.

GATE 2

Capital after a result

A larger round is rational only after the bench has passed, or after a failed run has produced a specific, funded revision. Use of proceeds then shifts from the cell to a prototype module and paid operator design reviews.

GATE 3

A pilot is a customer, not a slide

Site work comes after a developer can see measured water, energy, and noise. Until then, conversations are diagnostic. A pilot that starts to manufacture evidence is the wrong pilot.
